# Sales Call Script

Lines wrapped in **double asterisks** are word-for-word. Everything else is a framework the closer runs in their own words. Follow the script to the highest percentage possible: it lets the closer show skill inside a process with a proven track record.

## PHASE 1: AGENDA SET (scripted, 30-45 seconds)
Goal: get agreement on the call structure and set expectations.

**"Sweet. So here's what I want to do today. I'm going to ask you some questions about your goals and what's been going on, so I can give you some personalized advice. Then I'll break down exactly what we offer for people who are serious about making real progress. And if it makes sense for you, we'll talk about how to get started at the end. Sound fair enough?"**

Key points: get a YES before moving forward; set expectations (consultation, not hard sell, you are the expert); make it about them, not you; keep it under 45 seconds.

## PHASE 2: DISCOVERY / FACT FINDING (framework, 8-12 minutes)
Goal: uncover emotional drivers and identify real problems. Discovery is a scriptless framework treated as a fact-finding checklist: the information gathered is always the same. Get deeper and deeper as you go; never accept surface-level answers.

### Initial motivation (1-2 min)
"Perfect. So first things first. What actually motivated you to take time out of your day to schedule a call with us? Why us, why now?"
Find basic logistics (test date, type of test, basic goal). Listen for, then probe: surface-level goal; timing (why now, not 6 months ago?); urgency level.

### Current situation (1-2 min)
"Awesome, thanks for sharing that. So help me understand where you're at right now? What does a typical week look like for you when it comes to training and nutrition?"
Listen for, then probe: current habits; consistency level; what they're already doing; pain points in their system.

### Future pacing (2-3 min)
"Got it. So I want you to take me 12 months into the future. What does your ideal scenario look like? Where are you, how do you feel? Run me through it."
Listen for, then probe: specific, tangible outcomes (not vague goals); emotional language (how they'll feel, not just how they'll look); life impact (relationships, career, confidence).

### Probing (2-3 min)
"I hear you. So let me dig a little deeper here. What is it you're hoping that will bring you? Like, how will that affect other areas of your life?"
Listen for: the real "why" (deeper than the surface goal); emotional triggers (fear, pride, responsibility, legacy); life circumstances (kids, career, health).
Get DEEP. Keep asking "why" until you hit emotion: first why = surface goal; second why = reason for the goal; third why = emotional driver (THIS is what you want).

### Fear factor: the snap back (1-2 min)
"So if nothing changes and you're in the exact same place 12 months from now, how would you feel about that?"
Listen for: fear, regret, disappointment; impact on relationships, health, self-image; urgency and commitment level.

### Past attempts (2-3 min)
"So what have you tried so far to get to [Goal]?"
Listen for: what they've tried; why it didn't work; what they learned.
Dig deeper: "Was it a knowledge thing, a consistency thing, or something else?"

## PHASE 3: PROBLEM LABELING (scripted, 1-2 minutes)
Goal: summarize their situation and get agreement. One of the most important parts of the process: it shows the prospect you really understand them. Struggling to deliver this means discovery was poor, and that is unacceptable.

**"Okay, so just to make sure I've got this right. You're currently [Current Situation], you want to get to [Desired Situation], so that you can [Deep Root Cause], but the biggest thing holding you back from all that has been [Core Problem], does that sound about right?"**

Key points: USE THEIR WORDS; be specific and empathetic; get confirmation.

## PHASE 4: TRANSITION TO PITCH (scripted, 1-2 minutes)
Goal: create curiosity and get permission to present the solution.

**"Based on everything you've shared with me today, [Name], I have something that I think would do that for you. Let me know if you'd like me to tell you about it."**

Then SHUT UP. They will say yes. Hesitancy here is a CLEAR INDICATOR of a poor discovery.
Key points: make them ask for the pitch; use strategic silence; don't push.

## PHASE 5: THE PITCH (scripted with discovery callouts, 3-4 minutes)
Goal: present the solution so it feels custom and relevant. Every point must feel based on their specific situation.

**"Awesome. So here's what we do. We break everything down into four pillars: Fitness, Nutrition, Accountability, Balance."**

- Fitness: a custom workout plan built specifically for you, not a generic program. Based on your schedule, your job, your life situation, because what works for someone else might not work for you. [REFERENCE THEIR SITUATION: "Like with your [kids/job/schedule], we're going to make sure this fits YOUR life."]
- Nutrition: same thing. A dedicated nutrition coach who creates a custom meal plan based on what you actually like to eat, not a boring chicken-and-broccoli plan. Sustainable. [REFERENCE THEIR SITUATION: "We'll work with your [wife's food habits/schedule/preferences]."]
- Accountability: this is the big one. A dedicated one-on-one coach who checks in on you, sees if you're logging your workouts and meals, and calls you out if you're slacking. Because you need someone in your corner who cares about your success. [REFERENCE THEIR EMOTIONAL DRIVER: "Someone who's going to keep you on track toward [their deep goal]."]
- Balance: what most programs miss. We teach you how to adjust when life happens, because life WILL happen: you'll get sick, you'll have a vacation, work will get crazy. You learn to navigate that without losing your progress. [REFERENCE THEIR SITUATION: "With your [kids/job/schedule], this is crucial."]
- Everything is built inside our custom app, so it's all in one place. No juggling apps. Clean, simple, designed to get you to [their emotional goal] as fast as possible.

**"Now, after hearing that, what part do you think would help you the most?"**

Key points: customize to their specific pain points; reference their discovery answers throughout; transformation, not features; tie back to the emotional driver; end with a question, not a statement.

## PHASE 6: CLOSE (framework, 3-5 minutes)
Goal: get commitment and move them to payment.

### Gauge interest
"Cool, so where would you like to go from here?"
Listen for: are they interested; do they have objections; are they ready to move forward.

### Commitment tie-down
**"So here's the deal. Your goal is to [Goal], right? That's awesome, and we can get you there. But obviously what you've been doing isn't working, so we're going to need to work with you closely. And we are 100% committed to getting you to that goal. So I need a 100% commitment from you as well. Sound fair enough?"**
Key points: restate their goal; acknowledge the problem; ask for commitment; make it a two-way street.

### Price
**"Sweet, so to get you to that goal, the program for you is our 52/24/12 week program. The investment for that is just $7k."**

If objections are present, HANDLE THEM. Mindset: "You are either hanging up on me, or closing." Then close and onboard.
